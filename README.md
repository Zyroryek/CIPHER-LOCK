# ZERO//DAY — TECH ESCAPE COMPETITION EXAM PORTAL

> **Tagline:** *One Breach. Three Crises. Zero Second Chances.*

A minimalist, step-by-step cybersecurity examination and tournament platform for college events.

---

## 1. Initial Access Gateway

On launching the application, users are presented with a clean choice:
- **Participants Login:**
  - Students log in using their credentials given by the organizers (e.g. `student1` / `pass1`, `student2` / `pass2`, etc.).
  - They immediately begin **Round 1**.
- **Organiser Login:**
  - Organizers access the proctoring dashboard using the master passkey: **`@123`**.
  - All admin controls, secret answer keys, and shortlisting controls are hidden from participants.

---

## 2. Strict Step-by-Step Round Separation (Never Shown Together)

Rounds are strictly mutually exclusive and presented one by one:

1. **Round 1 Examination (with Interactive Office Map):**
   - Features the **Interactive Isometric Office Floor Plan Map** with 5 selectable sectors (Workstation 04, Rogue USB, East Turnstiles, CMOS Clock Stomp, Corridor Blind Spot).
   - 4 clear, minimalist forensics questions with radio-button selections.
   - **Round 2 is completely hidden and not rendered.**
   - Once submitted, participants enter the **Waiting Screen**:
     > *"Round 1 Examination Submitted. Status: Under Review by Event Organisers. Please wait while results are published."*

2. **Organiser Shortlisting for Round 2:**
   - In the Organiser Console (Passkey: `@123`), the organizer inspects all Round 1 submissions and scores.
   - Clicking **"Qualify for Round 2"** instantly promotes the student.

3. **Round 2 Examination (with AI-Generated Surveillance CCTV Video):**
   - Automatically unlocks only for the students shortlisted by the organizer.
   - **Surveillance CCTV Video Player with Real Camera Feeds:**
     - `CAM 01`: Main Entrance & Turnstiles (21:08:14 Intruder badge swipe)
     - `CAM 02`: Server Room Corridor (21:12:30 Optical diffuser lens blinding)
     - `CAM 03`: Workstation 04 (21:24:02 Rear USB keystroke injection)
     - Working Play/Pause, scrubber timeline, speed toggles, and scene jump buttons.
   - 4 video observation questions directly grounded in the video footage.
   - Once submitted, participants enter the **Grand Finals Waiting Screen**.

4. **Organiser Shortlisting for Round 3 (Grand Finals):**
   - The organizer reviews Round 2 video assessment scores and clicks **"Qualify for Round 3"**.

5. **Round 3 Examination (Grand Finals):**
   - Suspect profile dossiers and final forensic deduction questions.
   - Upon completion, generates the official Certified Incident Dossier with score accreditation out of 300 points!
